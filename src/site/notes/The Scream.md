# The Scream

> [!info] Shortform
> The 2665 catastrophe, known colloquially as *The Scream* is a hyper catastrophe that whiped out the entire interstellar and intrastellar infrastructure of [[Database/5 - Flora & Fauna/Human\|mankind]]. This led to a 600 year dark age.
