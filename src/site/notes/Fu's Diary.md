### 1140/05/14   —   3320/04/20
##### 耕作日  /  Boon
Spent the day with Song. Walked around the Mazeway. Large power outage as we went into the holo theatre for our anniversary. Bummer...

### 1440/06/03   —   3320/04/27
##### 集日  /  Sept
Witnessing more power outages. Song Ya has a nagging feeling something's up with the grid. I've also been picking up some rumours that the upper districts aren't facing difficulties. Unsurprising, but worth investigating.

### date
##### day

A new head of engineering infrastructure has been elected. Some Gooddeeds buffoon with friends in real estate who is just about competent enough to clothe himself. Overheard rumors that the headquarters will be moved to Foamheart commons. The local building is apparently growing a kind of fungus that is damaging the foundations of the building. Never heard of that happening in this city before.

### date
##### day

Went to speak to the representative to verify the information. Got shut down and requested to leave. As I informed them I wouldn't and we deserve answers, they called security on me. What is going on??

### date
##### day

Woke up to an aggravated Song-Ya. Says it's not worth putting our reputation at risk over some conspiracy. Sometimes I wonder if it's better to just roll with the punches and look the other way, but these outages are affecting local care houses, infirmaries, food banks. Sorry Song, I can't not look.
