# Machine Souls

Machine Souls are manufactured entities from pre-scream times. Their conciousness, contained in a quantum core, can be either bound or unbound - destined to perform certain tasks or to live as a free artificial soul. No known machine souls exists in the Linyi Beta sector and knowledge of these entities comes solely from historic texts.

## Quantum Cores

The hypothesis is that a machine soul is essentially its *quantum memory core*. This fabulously complex artifact is roughly the size of a man’s fist and usually appears to be a transparent, diamond-like stone that glows with a soft inner light, though other forms do exist. The fabrication of a memory core is a heroic effort of science largely beyond core worlds and very difficult even for baseline TL5 engineering. Cores provide the basic cognitive and memory functions for the AI’s mind. Without being attached to additional sensory and processing hardware, the AIlies “dreaming” within its core, unable to perceive the external world.
