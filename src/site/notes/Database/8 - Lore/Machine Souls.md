Machine Souls are manufactured entities from pre-scream times. Their conciousness, contained in a quantum core, can be either bound or unbound - destined to perform certain tasks or to live as a free artificial soul. No known machine souls exists in the Linyi Beta sector and knowledge of these entities comes solely from historic texts.
